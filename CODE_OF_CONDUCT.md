# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Axquen community aims to be welcoming, constructive, and useful to players
with different backgrounds and levels of technical experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Assume good intent while discussing disagreements.
- Give actionable, relevant feedback.
- Help others without demanding personal information.
- Respect maintainers' time and project boundaries.
- Credit creators and respect licenses.
- Keep discussions focused and suitable for a public technical community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, hate speech, threats, or targeted abuse.
- Doxxing or sharing private information.
- Spam, scams, impersonation, or malicious links.
- Posting malware, credentials, tokens, or stolen material.
- Deliberately disrupting support channels.
- Pressuring others to bypass account authentication or licensing systems.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, restrict participation, or
remove users from project spaces when necessary. Serious or repeated violations
may result in a permanent ban. Report concerns privately through the official
Axquen Discord: https://discord.gg/StdYxkEXRc

This code applies to project discussions, issue reports, pull requests, and
official community spaces.
