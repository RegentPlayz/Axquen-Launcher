# Privacy and Data Practices

## Important status

This document is intentionally conservative. The repository information
available for this package does not establish Axquen's complete data
collection, telemetry, analytics, retention, or third-party sharing practices.
The items below are marked **Developer confirmation required** where the
implementation or policy has not been verified.

This page is not a substitute for a formal privacy notice published by the
developer.

## Account information

**Developer confirmation required.** Confirm what account identifiers or
profile information Axquen processes, where that information is stored, and
whether any information is transmitted to Axquen-operated or third-party
services.

## Authentication

**Developer confirmation required.** Confirm the authentication flow, token
handling, storage location, token protection, and any authentication providers
or endpoints involved. Users should never disclose passwords, tokens, or
session files to support.

## Local configuration

**Developer confirmation required.** Confirm which settings, preferences,
account metadata, and launcher configuration files are stored locally and
whether any are synchronized.

## Game instance data

Minecraft worlds, mods, configuration, screenshots, and logs may exist in
local instance folders. **Developer confirmation required** for Axquen-specific
storage paths, backups, synchronization, or access practices.

## Crash logs and diagnostics

Axquen includes crash-detection and diagnostic functionality. **Developer
confirmation required** for what diagnostic data is collected, whether logs
are transmitted, what information is included, and whether users are asked
before sharing.

## Third-party services

The product information identifies Modrinth and CurseForge source selectors
and Microsoft account support. **Developer confirmation required** for exact
network requests, data exchanged, service providers, and applicable third-party
privacy terms.

## Data retention and deletion

**Developer confirmation required.** Confirm retention periods, deletion
controls, backup behavior, and how users can request deletion of information
held by developer-operated services.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the official Axquen
Discord: https://discord.gg/StdYxkEXRc

Do not send passwords, authentication tokens, or private session files.
